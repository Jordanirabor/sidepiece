Now I understand the real vision for **Side Piece**. It's not primarily a relationship simulator. It's an **adult social dating game built around sexual exploration, attraction, hookups, multiple partners, money, and reputation.**

The key difference is that players shouldn't simply choose from a menu of scripted dates. They should create a character, specify what they're interested in, enter a social environment, and discover other players who are looking for compatible experiences.

That changes how I'd design the entire game.

# Side Piece — The Core Game

## 1. The player journey

**1. Create your character**

Appearance, outfit, personality and preferences

**2. Enter the world**

Spawn into a location where compatible players are looking to meet

**3. Meet and attract**

Discover profiles, express interest, match and chat

**4. Date, hook up or explore**

Agree on an experience and visit a private location together

**5. Earn, rate and progress**

Earn game currency, increase your reputation and grow your body count

## 2. Character creation: what are you looking for?

This should be more than choosing a face and an outfit. Players define the kind of experiences they want to explore.


> 🖼️ _Reference image search: stylized 3D attractive adult male and female customizable game avatars fashionable urban nightlife character creator, stylized 3D fashionable adult characters Lagos nightlife game art_


Each player sets:

- **Appearance:** face, body type, hairstyle and fashion.
- **Lifestyle:** personality, wealth level and preferred social scene.
- **Relationship style:** single, casual dating, multiple partners or committed relationships.
- **Interests:** the kinds of dates and consensual adult romantic encounters they want.
- **Intent:** meet people, flirt, date, find a casual partner or pursue a longer relationship.

These preferences help determine which characters and players appear nearby.

## 3. The world: spawn into opportunity

This is potentially the game's strongest feature.

Rather than opening a static menu of people, the player enters a lively location: a lounge, nightclub, rooftop party, beach party or apartment social event.


> 🖼️ _Reference image search: stylized 3D game environment upscale Lagos rooftop lounge at night neon city lights, stylized 3D game environment modern Lagos nightclub adults fashion socializing, stylized 3D game environment luxury apartment living room night city view_


The player sees other adult characters moving around, socializing and checking people out.

Each character has a visible profile:

- Name and avatar.
- Style and social rating.
- Relationship status, if they choose to disclose it.
- What they're looking for.
- Whether they're interested in the player.

The matching system prioritizes **mutual compatibility**, not just proximity.

A player looking for a casual encounter shouldn't constantly be matched with people looking for exclusive relationships. That makes the world feel more relevant and reduces wasted interactions.

The gameplay then follows five steps:

1. Approach or message someone.
2. Build attraction through conversation and choices.
3. Establish mutual interest and agree on what happens next.
4. Go on a date or move to a private location.
5. Complete the encounter, receive feedback and update your stats.

Intimacy can be represented through stylized, non-graphic animation and scene transitions. The important gameplay is the interaction, consent, outcome and progression.

One important distinction: body count should measure unique partners, while total encounters measures repeat encounters. A player's broader reputation could also reflect partner feedback, compatibility and reliability.

That gives players more than one way to build a name for themselves.

## 6. The game economy

Players earn fictional currency through completed activities, partner ratings and achievements. They spend it on outfits, dates, gifts, venue access and lifestyle upgrades.

The simplest progression system is:

**Social rating → Better opportunities → More encounters → Rewards → New experiences**

I'd avoid making body count alone determine the player's earnings. Otherwise, players have little incentive to explore other mechanics, and the game becomes repetitive.

Instead, rewards could reflect a combination of successful encounters, mutual ratings, social reputation and optional challenges.

## 7. What makes it addictive?

I'd focus on three things:

- **Discovery:** Who is here, and who is interested in me?
- **Choice:** Do I pursue this person, another person or someone I've already met?
- **Progression:** How will this encounter change my reputation, relationships and opportunities?

Multiple partners and cheating fit naturally into this world. Players should be free to pursue different relationship styles, with consequences emerging when expectations conflict—not because the game forces everyone into monogamy.

## My recommendation for the first playable version

Don't build a huge city or sophisticated artificial intelligence yet.

Start with:

1. Character creation and preference selection.
2. One shared social location.
3. A small roster of adult characters and compatible players.
4. Matching, likes and messaging.
5. A date-to-private-location flow.
6. Non-graphic intimacy outcomes.
7. Body count, ratings and fictional currency.
8. A profile showing progress and reputation.

**One important product decision remains:** should *Side Piece* be primarily a real-time multiplayer game where actual players meet each other, or a single-player social simulation where players interact with AI-controlled characters?

That decision affects almost everything: matchmaking, chat, animation, server architecture, safety and development cost. My instinct is to build the core around real player-to-player encounters if that is the social experience you want, while using scripted scenes and NPCs to keep the world lively when activity is low.
